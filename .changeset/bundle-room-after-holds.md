`dependency-security`: a row a closed issue vetoes, or an open issue already proposes, no longer takes room in a transitive bundle whose body is full. The rows after it move up. Before, a full bundle whose leading rows were all vetoed filed nothing on every scan, and the rows left out never came in. It takes a body over 60,000 characters to matter, which is several hundred rows.
